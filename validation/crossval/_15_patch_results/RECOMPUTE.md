## Why the leg must be re-solved

Both conductors are now DECLARED sheets — zero-thickness PEC `Box`es at
`z_sub_lo` and `z_sub_hi` — and the feed spans the full substrate from the
ground sheet plane. Three things change in the structure the solver builds:

1. the ground no longer owns the cell below the substrate floor, so the vacuum
   cell that inflated the cavity's electrical thickness by +55.0 % is gone
   (it was masked, not removed, by `two_plane=True`);
2. the patch no longer occupies a cell, so nothing can grow a wall at
   `k_patch + 1` = 11.9062 mm;
3. the port starts ON the ground sheet plane and reaches the patch plane
   instead of stopping one cell short — galvanic instead of capacitive-only
   (#556 / #929).

The committed `rfx.json` therefore describes a different board. It also
predates `stack_check.n_distinct_eps`, so `compare()`'s stack gate fails on
it BY DESIGN until it is regenerated — a leg that never measured the property
is not evidence that the property holds.

## Which keys change

a fresh solve. The ones that carry a claim:

* `f_primary_hz` / `f_harminv_hz`, `q_harminv`, `gain_dbi`, `settle_db`,
  `max_abs_s11`, `f_dip_hz`, `s11_dip_db`, `freqs_hz`/`s11_mag`;

  `ground_realization` goes `"two_plane"` → `"sheet"`;

  improvement yet. Preflight has not been migrated on this branch (design note
  §6, "Not yet implemented"), so it still measures metal from the primal CELL
  mask and cannot see a sheet. Expect it to report the two zero-thickness
  Boxes as a non-uniform-mesh advisory, to emit the
  `_assemble_materials … no pec_sheets/pec_wires collector` warning, and to
  claim the feed is "inside PEC geometry" — that last one is #929 defect 1 and
  is the preflight group's to fix, not cv15's. Do NOT treat those lines as a
  cv15 regression.

`patch_compare.png` is regenerated by the compare step.

## Pre-declared expectation (written BEFORE the run)

The critic's reading, recorded so the result can contradict it: under
sheet+sheet at the same planes the patch's realized edge set and cell materials
match the #768 build, so `f_primary` should move only through the feed change
(full-span, galvanic). A large shift in f0 is a FINDING, not a success — the
#556 advisory says the old feed coupled capacitively, which loads the cavity,
so a small DOWNWARD shift in the resonance and a deeper `s11_dip_db` are the
expected direction. The gates that must still pass: f0 vs openEMS <= 8 %,
settling <= -40 dB, passivity max|S11| <= 1.05 on both legs, broadside D within
3 dB, and the stack gate. If the stack gate fails, the leg is not evidence and
nothing may be quoted from it.
